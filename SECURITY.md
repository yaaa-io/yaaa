# Security

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security issues privately, not in public issues or on Discord:

- **Preferred:** GitHub's private reporting. Use **Report a vulnerability** on this repo's [Security tab](https://github.com/yaaa-io/yaaa/security). Only you and the yaaa team can see the report.
- **Or by email:** **support@nxis.ai**.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, what an attacker could do with it, and the yaaa version (`yaaa --version`). If you're unsure whether something is a security issue, report it privately anyway.

## What to expect

- We'll acknowledge your report within **three business days**, and keep you updated until it's resolved.
- We'll tell you when it's fixed, and credit you in the published advisory if you'd like.
- Please give us reasonable time to fix it before you disclose it publicly. We'll agree a date with you; 90 days is our default.

## Scope

In scope: the yaaa command-line tool, its agent skills, the MCP server, and the API contract in this repo, and the yaaa service they talk to (api.yaaa.io, the web desk, and yaaa.io).

Out of scope: the stores yaaa buys from, and Shopify itself (report those to them); denial-of-service and load testing; social engineering; and reports from automated scanners without a demonstrated issue.

## Testing rules

- Use only accounts you created yourself. Don't access, change, or delete other people's data; if you come across any, stop and tell us.
- Don't disrupt the service or the stores yaaa buys from: no load or denial-of-service tests, and no automated buying.
- Don't complete real purchases you don't intend to keep, and never use payment details that aren't yours.

## Safe harbor

If you follow this policy in good faith, we won't pursue or support legal action against you for your research, and we'll treat it as authorized. If someone else brings a claim over research that followed this policy, we'll make it known that it was authorized.

## Bug bounty

There's no paid bug bounty yet. We're grateful for every report, and we credit reporters who'd like it.

# Code of conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) in this repo's issues and in the YAAA Discord: be respectful, assume good intent, and keep discussion about yaaa.

Report unacceptable behavior to **support@nxis.ai**. Reports are handled privately, and NxisAI may remove content or block people who don't follow these rules.
